# Earnings Before Interest and Taxes

Indicador financeiro que mede o lucro operacional de uma empresa antes da dedução de juros e impostos, revelando a sua capacidade de gerar receita através das suas operações principais.

# Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Deductions and Amortization

It is a financial metric used to measure a company's operating profitability and performance by showing earnings before non-operational expenses are deducted. By excluding interest, taxes, and non-cash charges like depreciation and amortization, EBITDA provides a clearer view of a company's core business operations and cash flow generation potential
